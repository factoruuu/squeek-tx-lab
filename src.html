📡 Ping SQK — a safe first transaction

Sends approve(your own address, 0) to the $SQK contract from your wallet. It moves no tokens and grants no spendable allowance (the amount is zero and the spender is yourself) — it simply records a real transaction from your address to a verified Squeekchain contract, the same first step Genesis members use for the Tester badge. You only pay the small Sepolia gas fee.
